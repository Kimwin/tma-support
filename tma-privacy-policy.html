Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 29, 2026

TMA ("we", "us", "our") provides anime and manga tracking features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account and profile data: AniList account information you authorize us to access (for example username, avatar, list and activity data exposed by AniList APIs).
  • App usage and diagnostics: analytics events for feature usage and performance, plus crash and error diagnostics.
  • Subscription and entitlement data: subscription status and entitlement metadata required to unlock paid features.
  • Optional preferences and local state: in-app preferences and progression/streak state used by TMA features.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app functionality (tracking, insights, badges, progression).
  • Sync and display your authorized AniList data.
  • Process and validate subscription access.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and product quality.
  • Provide support and troubleshoot issues.

3. Services and Processors

TMA may use third-party services to operate the app, including:

  • AniList (authorized account and list data)
  • RevenueCat (subscription infrastructure)
  • Supabase (backend functions and storage)
  • Firebase (analytics and crash reporting)

These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Notifications

TMA supports optional notifications (for example streak reminders and app alerts). You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for app operation, support, legal compliance, and legitimate business purposes. Retention periods may vary by data type and provider.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke AniList authorization by logging out.
  • Disable app notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Request subscription restoration via app controls.
  • Contact us for privacy requests.

7. Children

TMA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International Processing

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on provider infrastructure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: trackma.app@mail.com.
