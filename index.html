WHAT IS TMA?

TMA is a fast, beautifully designed tracker for anime and manga — built for iPhone. Sign in with your AniList account to sync your library, track episodes and chapters, discover new titles, and join community discussions. You can also explore as a guest with no account needed.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I sync my anime and manga library?
Tap the Sign in with AniList button on the login screen. This will take you to the AniList login page where you can authenticate. Once done, your full library will be available in TMA automatically.
Do I need an account to use TMA?
No — you can explore the app as a guest without signing in. An AniList account is only required if you want to sync your personal lists and track your progress.
My library isn't showing after signing in. What should I do?
Try signing out and tapping the AniList login button again. Make sure you completed the full login flow on the AniList page. If the issue persists, reach out using the contact options below.
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
There's a quick toggle button in the top-left of the Home screen — tap it to switch between light and dark mode instantly.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
You can reach us via email or X/Twitter below, or use the Contact button in your Profile inside the app — it's the quickest way to send feedback directly.
